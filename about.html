The Story Behind WithMomma

Built by a midwife, for the professionals who support families.

WithMomma was created by [FOUNDER NAME], a certified midwife, who understands first-hand how much time and care goes into educating and supporting families throughout pregnancy.

WithMomma exists to give midwives, doulas, pregnancy health coaches, and maternal wellness professionals a thoughtful starting point — editable, professional-grade resources that save preparation time and elevate the client experience.

Every resource is designed with a midwifery-informed perspective, drawing on reputable maternal-health guidance and a clear, calm communication style. The goal is simple: to help professionals educate, support, and empower the families in their care.

Our Approach

A midwifery-informed perspective on pregnancy education.

What We Create

Editable digital resources — ebooks, guides, planners, and printable educational materials — designed for client education and professional use.

Who We Support

Midwives, doulas, pregnancy health coaches, maternal wellness professionals, pregnancy educators, and related professionals.

How We Approach Education

Clear, calm, evidence-informed, and respectful. We design resources that make it easier to communicate information in a supportive way.
