FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WithMomma?

WithMomma creates thoughtfully designed pregnancy education resources for professionals who support families throughout the pregnancy journey. Resources are editable, professional-grade, and designed to save time while elevating client education.

Who are WithMomma resources designed for?

WithMomma resources are primarily designed for midwives, doulas, pregnancy health coaches, maternal wellness professionals, and pregnancy educators. Some resources may also be appropriate for pregnant women and expecting families.

Are the templates editable?

Yes. WithMomma templates are editable in Canva. You can customize text, colors, and layout to match your branding and your clients' needs.

Do I need Canva Pro?

Most WithMomma resources are designed to be compatible with Canva Free. Any Pro-only elements will be clearly noted on the individual product page.

What does PLR mean?

PLR stands for Private Label Rights. Products that include PLR come with the rights specified in the individual license included with that product. Please review the license included with each product before purchasing.

Can I use the resources with clients?

In most cases, yes — WithMomma resources are designed for professional use with clients. The specific rights depend on the individual product license. Please review the license included with each product before purchasing.

Are these resources medical advice?

No. WithMomma resources are provided for general educational purposes and are not a substitute for individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or care. Please speak with your doctor, midwife, or qualified healthcare professional about your individual circumstances.

Where can I purchase WithMomma products?

WithMomma products are available through our Etsy shop. Visit the Shop page to explore the collection.
