About This Resource

A professional pregnancy nutrition guide — ready in minutes.

Are you a pregnancy coach, midwife, doula, nutritionist, or women's wellness professional who wants to provide trusted, professional guidance about healthy eating during pregnancy — without spending hours creating it from scratch?

This Pregnancy Nutrition Guide Template is a beautifully designed, fully editable Canva mini eBook created by a midwifery professional. It's a done-for-you, PLR-ready resource that helps you educate, inspire, and empower expecting mothers to nourish themselves and their babies confidently throughout pregnancy.

Inside, you'll find clear, evidence-based information on what to eat, what to avoid, and why it matters — healthy habits that support both mother and baby.
