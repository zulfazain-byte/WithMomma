About This Resource

A respectful, evidence-based guide to intimacy in pregnancy.

Are you a pregnancy coach, midwife, doula, or women's wellness professional looking to provide clear, supportive guidance about intimacy during pregnancy — without spending countless hours researching, writing, and designing it yourself?

This Sex and Intimacy in Pregnancy Guide is a beautifully designed, fully editable Canva template crafted with a professional, compassionate approach. It's a done-for-you, PLR-ready resource that helps you educate, reassure, and empower clients as they navigate physical, emotional, and relational changes during pregnancy.

Inside, you'll find warm, evidence-based content that helps normalize concerns around libido changes, comfort, communication, emotional connection, and sexual wellbeing — presented in a way that feels supportive, stigma-free, and easy to understand.

With this template, you can save time, elevate your authority, and offer trusted, high-quality education that truly serves your clients. Simply add your branding, adjust fonts and colors, and your guide will be ready to publish, share, or sell in minutes.
