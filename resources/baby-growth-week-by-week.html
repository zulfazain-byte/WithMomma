About This Resource

Turn complex development into something warm and understandable.

Are you a pregnancy coach, midwife, doula, or women's wellness professional looking to beautifully guide expecting mothers through their baby's development week-by-week — without creating everything from scratch?

This Baby Growth Week by Week Canva Template is a thoughtfully designed, fully editable resource crafted with a gentle, educational approach. Created by a midwifery professional, this PLR-ready template helps you deliver clear, comforting, and evidence-based insights into your baby's growth throughout pregnancy.

Inside, you'll guide mothers through each stage of pregnancy with simple explanations on how pregnancy is measured, how their baby is developing week by week, and how big their baby is becoming using familiar and easy-to-visualize comparisons. It's designed to turn complex medical concepts into something warm, understandable, and reassuring.

Perfect for building trust with your audience, enhancing your brand, and offering meaningful value, this template allows you to simply customize, brand, and publish with ease.

Do I need Canva Pro?

No. This template is designed to be fully editable in the Canva Free version. No Pro subscription is required.

What file formats and sizes are included?

You receive a PDF file with an instant access link to the Canva template, in three eBook sizes: A5, A4, and US Letter. You can export the final product from Canva as PDF, JPG, PNG, or other supported formats.

What does the eBook cover?

The eBook covers three main topics: how pregnancy is measured (from early development to due date estimation), how your baby develops week by week, and the size of your baby each week using easy-to-visualize fruit comparisons.

Are the fruit-size comparisons realistic?

The fruit comparisons are designed to be gentle, memorable visual aids — not medical measurements. They are intended to help expecting mothers feel connected to their baby's growth week by week. Actual measurements vary by individual pregnancy and should always be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Can I use this with clients?

Yes. Your purchase includes a single-user, non-transferable PLR license that allows you to use the template and the final exported product with clients in your business, programs, and services.
