Do I need Canva Pro?

No. This template is designed to be fully editable in the Canva Free version. No Pro subscription is required.

What file formats and sizes are included?

You receive a PDF file with an instant access link to the Canva template, in three sizes: A5, A4, and US Letter. You can export the final product from Canva as PDF, JPG, PNG, or other supported formats.

What does the checklist cover?

The checklist covers when to start packing, what to bring for mom (3 pages), for baby (2 pages), and for partner (1 page), plus a page on what to check before leaving and a condensed printable quick checklist.

Can I use this with clients?

Yes. Your purchase includes a single-user, non-transferable PLR license that allows you to use the template and the final exported product with clients in your business, programs, and services.

Can I customize the resource with my branding?

Absolutely. Everything is editable — replace photos, adjust fonts, change colors, add or remove pages, translate into your own language, and add your own content.

What does PLR mean?

PLR stands for Private Label Rights. Upon purchase you receive a single-user, non-transferable PLR license. This means you can rebrand and use the product with clients, and sell the final non-editable export. However, you may not resell the editable template or share the Canva link. All copyrights remain with WithMomma.

Can I resell the editable Canva template?

No. You may sell the final, exported, non-editable file (e.g., PDF, JPG, PNG), but you may not resell the editable Canva template, share the Canva link, or transfer resale rights (PLR, RR, MRR).

No. Hospital and birth center policies vary widely by country and by facility. The checklist is a general, practical starting point — clients should always follow the specific guidance of their care team and their birth setting.
