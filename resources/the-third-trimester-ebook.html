About This Resource

A complete guide to the final stretch — ready in minutes.

Are you a pregnancy coach, midwife, doula, or women's wellness professional looking to support expecting mothers through the final stage of pregnancy — without spending hours creating content from scratch?

This Third Trimester eBook Canva Template is a beautifully designed, fully editable Canva resource created by a midwifery professional. It's a done-for-you (DFY), PLR-ready guide that helps you educate, reassure, and empower your clients as they approach birth with confidence and clarity.

Inside, you'll find clear, evidence-based guidance covering everything from weeks 28 to birth — from baby's rapid growth and positioning, to physical changes, emotional shifts, and preparation for labor. This guide is designed to help mothers feel informed, supported, and ready for the final stretch of pregnancy.

With this template, you can save time, elevate your professional branding, and deliver high-quality, trusted content your clients will truly value. Simply customize with your brand, adjust colors and fonts, and your third trimester guide will be ready to publish in minutes.
